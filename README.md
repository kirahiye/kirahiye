# Hi, I'm kirahiye

I make free, simple web projects: personality quizzes and unofficial game wikis.

## Quizzes

- [Dere Test](https://deretest.org/), a free anime personality quiz with 16 or 24 scenarios and 8 dere types. For entertainment, not a scientific assessment.
- [7 Deadly Sins Test](https://7deadly-sins-test.org/), a free quiz exploring the seven deadly sins through everyday statements. For entertainment and self-reflection, not a scientific or clinical assessment.

## Gaming wikis

- [Probably Stolen Wiki](https://probably-stolen.net/), an unofficial fan wiki with shop references, item prices, counterfeit guides, and calculators.
- [Melvor Idle 2 Wiki](https://melvor-idle-2-game.wiki/), an unofficial fan wiki with player guides, item and skill references, and calculators.
- [No Asteroids Wiki](https://noasteroidswiki.com/), an unofficial fan wiki with beginner guides, tools and ship weapons pages, co-op help, demo notes, and patch coverage.
- [Overflow Roblox Wiki](https://overflowroblox.wiki/), an unofficial fan wiki for [UPD] Overflow by kyo squad, with working codes, race and fighting style guides, and patch notes. Not affiliated with kyo squad or Roblox Corporation.
